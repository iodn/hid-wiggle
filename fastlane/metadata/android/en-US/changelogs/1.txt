## Changelog (v1.0)

* Initial HIDWiggle release
* Root-based USB HID mouse gadget activation through Linux ConfigFS
* Mouse jiggler dashboard with start/stop controls and live gadget status
* Movement modes: Micro-wiggle, Random drift, Square, Figure-8, and Pulse
* Live tuning for interval, amplitude, jitter, randomness, and human-like variation
* Scheduler with day/time windows, auto-stop, and pause rules
* USB identity settings for VID/PID, manufacturer, product, serial, and max power
* Test Mode for manual HID movement, click, and scroll validation
* Diagnostics and native logs for root, UDC, ConfigFS, and HID readiness troubleshooting
